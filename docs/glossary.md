# Glossary

Baseline
:   The crew member's preflight equilibrium score in a condition, the median of three trials.

CANNOT ASSESS
:   The result when a recording is invalid or session times are missing or out of order. Never GO.

CAUTION
:   The result when a score lies between the 75% line and the GO threshold, below the population floor, or when a
    single GO session awaits confirmation.

Centre of mass
:   The point at which the body's mass can be considered concentrated. Its sway is used to compute the equilibrium
    score.

Centre of pressure
:   The point of application of the ground-reaction force under the feet, recorded by the force plate.

Confirmatory session
:   A second GO session, at least 2 h after the first, required for an overall GO.

Equilibrium score (EQ)
:   100 × (1 − θ / 12.5°), where θ is the peak-to-peak anterior–posterior sway angle over 20 s. A score of 100
    indicates no sway.

Force plate
:   A platform that measures the forces and moments applied by a person standing on it.

GO
:   The result when two consecutive recorded sessions at least 2 h apart are GO in every condition. The egress
    decision rests with the crew surgeon.

GO threshold
:   The ratio to baseline a score must reach for GO: the larger of 0.75 × the test–retest margin and the
    population floor divided by the baseline.

NO-GO
:   The result after a fall or a score below 75% of baseline.

Population floor
:   The 5th percentile of healthy reference scores in the same condition and age group.

Ratio to baseline
:   The session score divided by the baseline in the same condition.

Recovery time constant (τ)
:   The time in which the remaining deficit falls to about 37% of its value, in the NASA crew recovery model.

Sensory Organization Test
:   NASA's force-plate balance test, with six conditions that alter visual and somatosensory information.

Sway-referenced support
:   A platform that tilts with the person's sway, so that ankle position gives little information about body
    orientation.

Test–retest margin (q95)
:   The 95th percentile of the ratio between two measurements of the same person when no true change has occurred.
