    Decision rules¶

    Rules within a condition¶

    For each condition, the rules are applied in numerical order, and the first rule that applies sets the result.

    Rule Condition Result
    1 Any trial invalid, fewer than three trials, or baseline missing or invalid CANNOT ASSESS
    2 Any trial ended in a fall or was stopped NO-GO
    3 \(r_c < 0.75\) NO-GO
    4 Session score below the healthy population floor \(P5_c\) CAUTION and referral
    5 \(r_c \ge q_c\) (GO threshold) GO
    6 Any other score CAUTION
    7 Overall result and two-session requirement see below

    Rule 3: the 75% line. Jain et al. (2010) regarded a decrement of less than 25% from a crew member's own preflight score as recovery. A score below 75% of baseline therefore yields NO-GO.

    Rule 4: the population floor. Jain et al. (2010) regarded a score below the 5th percentile of normative data as clinically abnormal. The floor \(P5_c\) is the 5th percentile of reference scores in the same condition and age group, from HBEDB participants without a reported disability, excluding reported labyrinthitis (see Validation). A crew member whose score is below the floor is referred to the crew surgeon, even when the score is close to their own baseline.

    The GO threshold¶

    A single test is noisy. A score just above 75% of baseline may come from a true score below it. The GO threshold therefore keeps the score at or above 75% of baseline after allowance for test–retest variability, and at or above the population floor:

    \[ q_c = \max\!\left(0.75\,q_{95,c},\; \frac{P5_c}{b_c}\right) \]

    The margin \(q_{95,c}\) is the 95th percentile of the ratio between the median of three retest trials and the median of three baseline trials when no true change has occurred. It is the larger of two estimates:

    • From data. Residuals of healthy HBEDB participants, by condition, age group and baseline tertile, resampled 200,000 times.
    • From the literature. The minimal detectable change of the head-shake Sensory Organization Test reported by Pang et al. (2011), 2.7 (younger) and 3.6 (older) points on the firm conditions and 16.2 and 22.7 points on the foam conditions. Each value, reported for a five-trial mean, is converted to a per-trial standard deviation, \(\mathrm{MDC}\sqrt{5}/(1.96\sqrt{2})\), and three-trial medians are simulated 100,000 times at each baseline level with independent, normally distributed errors.

    The literature margin is a transfer. Pang et al. measured five-trial means with head movements on firm and sway-referenced surfaces and divided age groups at 50 years. Here it is applied to three-trial medians with the head still, including foam, with the HBEDB age groups divided at 60 years. Taking the larger of the two margins is the cautious choice.

    If \(q_c \ge 1\), the GO threshold lies at or above baseline. A condition whose score is still below such a threshold cannot reach it under the recovery model, and no retest time is estimated. A condition already at or above its threshold needs no recovery.

    Example. For participant A on foam with eyes closed, \(b = 84.7\), \(q_{95} = 1.267\) and \(P5 = 77.1\), so \(q = \max(0.950, 0.910) = 0.950\). A score must reach 95% of baseline for GO.

    Published values and design choices¶

    Quantity Origin
    75% line Published: working definition of recovery (Jain et al. 2010)
    5th-percentile floor Published criterion (Jain et al. 2010), computed from HBEDB reference data
    Test–retest margin Computed from HBEDB data and from published values (Pang et al. 2011), with the transfer assumptions above
    Two sessions, at least 2 h apart Design choice
    Recovery model Published (Wood et al. 2015, Table III)

    Overall result (rule 7)¶

    • The overall result is that of the least favourable condition, in the order CANNOT ASSESS, NO-GO, CAUTION, GO.
    • A test with a condition that was not measured cannot yield GO and is limited to CAUTION.
    • Overall GO requires two consecutive GO tests in separate recorded sessions at least 2 h apart. A single GO session gives CAUTION with a request for a confirmatory session. Session times must be strictly increasing, otherwise the result is CANNOT ASSESS.
    • The retest estimate never yields GO. Only a measured session can.

    Recommended actions¶

    Situation Recommended action
    NO-GO Do not leave the vehicle unassisted. Retest at the estimated time, or refer to the crew surgeon if no time can be estimated or a fall occurred
    CAUTION after one GO session A confirmatory test in a separate session at least 2 h later
    CAUTION for any other reason Retest at the estimated time, or refer to the crew surgeon
    CANNOT ASSESS Repeat the test with valid recordings, or correct the session times
    GO No further testing is required under the decision rules. The egress decision rests with the crew surgeon
